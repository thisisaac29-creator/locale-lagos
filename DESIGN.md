# Locale visual system
Editorial restaurant site, extended from the existing serif/sans and arch composition. Supplied photography and menus are visual authority. Detailed evidence, palette derivation and asset decisions: docs/brand-notes.md.

Colors: cream #F0E6D7, paper #FAF6EE, sand #EBD1AE, clay #B8AF9E, terracotta #7F4224, umber #423327, ink #121111, leaf #455232. Type: self-hosted Cormorant and DM Sans. Body 16px; comfortable line length; display tracking -0.035em. Patterns: original tile triangles, chevrons, weave, rings and short marks; no named textile attribution. Patterns remain framing accents, at most two families in a viewport. Solid text grounds, photographic subject priority, fixed media ratios, reduced-motion alternative.

Home/About persuade through real rooms and food; Gallery lets visitors explore source photography and silent films; Menu prioritizes readable source-exact data. Private dining uses a weave surface until a room photograph is confirmed.
